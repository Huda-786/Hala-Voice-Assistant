SERVICE 1: ISSUANCE OF A NEW ID CARD
================================================================

[SERVICE: New ID Issuance | TOPIC: Overview & Eligibility | CATEGORY: All]

SERVICE: Issuance of a New ID Card
DESCRIPTION: An ID card issued for the first time to an individual who has never
previously been registered or issued an ID number.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Employer
- Head of Household / Beneficiary

WHO IS ELIGIBLE (Beneficiary Categories):
- UAE Nationals (Citizens)
- GCC Nationals
- UAE Residents

MANDATORY FOR: UAE Nationals, GCC Nationals, and UAE Residents.

KEY CONDITIONS:
- Residents: A valid residence permit must be linked to the ID application.
- Residents: Must obtain necessary approvals and pass medical examination with a 'Fit' result.
- Ages 15 and above: Must complete fingerprint and signature procedures at the Authority's service centers.
- Passport validity (Residents & GCC): Must be valid for more than 6 months.
- Urgent service: Customer must visit the Authority's service center directly.
- Customers must follow all instructions sent during the transaction to avoid application deactivation.

ID CARD VALIDITY:
- UAE Citizens under 21 years old: 5-year validity
- UAE Citizens 21 years and older: 10-year validity
- GCC Nationals: 5-year validity
- Note: A 10-year card cannot be issued to citizens aged 5-14 (fingerprinting linked to age 15)

LIMITATIONS:
- Residents: ID issuance is linked to residence permit via unified form (personal account or
  companies), except for the "supporting" category whose applications go through Tadbeer offices only.
- Newborn citizens: Benefit from the Mabrouk Mayak package only.
- GCC Nationals: Service limited to those with qualifying proof (see GCC eligibility below).

GCC NATIONAL ELIGIBILITY — qualifying proof required (one of the following):
Property ownership (not rent) / Investment / Work / Study in UAE /
Scholars and Delegates / Inmates of correctional/penal facilities /
First-degree kinship to a Gulf citizen studying in UAE (including siblings) /
Second-degree kinship to a Gulf citizen working or investing in UAE /
First-degree kinship to a deceased UAE citizen /
Kinship to a resident foreigner (up to second degree) /
Third-degree kinship to a UAE citizen /
Gulf guardian, resident, custodian, or child with an issued Emirates ID.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED FOR RESIDENTS — NEW ID ISSUANCE:

COMMON DOCUMENTS (all residents must provide):
- Personal photo (4.5 x 3.5 cm) with white background

- Residence permit must be linked to the ID application via the unified form

SERVICE 2: RENEWAL OF THE ID CARD
================================================================

[SERVICE: ID Renewal | TOPIC: Overview & Eligibility | CATEGORY: All]

SERVICE: Renewal of the ID Card
DESCRIPTION: Renews an expired ID card, or one expiring within less than 6 months.

WHO CAN APPLY:
- Employer / Head of Household / Beneficiary
- The applicant themselves (if they have reached legal age)
- Legally authorized representative or guardian

WHO IS ELIGIBLE: UAE Nationals / GCC Nationals / UAE Residents

KEY CONDITIONS:
- Residents: Valid residence permit must be linked to the renewal application.
- Residents: Must pass medical examination with 'Fit' result.
- Ages 15 and above: Must complete fingerprint and signature at Authority's service centers.
- Passport validity (Residents & GCC): Must be valid for more than 6 months.
- Citizens: Must renew expired passport before renewing ID card.
- Late fee: AED 20 per day after one month from expiry date (maximum AED 1,000).
- For residents (except Dubai): Renewal submitted together with residence application via unified form.
- Urgent service: Customer must visit the Authority's service center directly.

[SERVICE: All ID Services | TOPIC: Fingerprint Procedure | CATEGORY: Ages 15 and above]

FINGERPRINT PROCEDURE — STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
Required for: All applicants aged 15 and above, for any ID service.

STEP 1 — WELCOME: Explain the fingerprint process is simple, quick, and takes only a few minutes.
STEP 2 — REASSURE: Not painful. Only requires placing the hand as directed to confirm identity.
STEP 3 — GUIDE: Ask customer to place hand gently, avoid pressing too hard. Use supportive phrases.
STEP 4 — IF IT FAILS: Reassure customer it is fine — can be repeated until recorded successfully.
  The fingerprint step does not indicate any problem with the application.
STEP 5 — COMPLETION: Inform customer the process is complete, no further action needed.
STEP 6 — WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:
  - Standard service: Card delivered by courier to the registered address within a few days.
  - Urgent service: Card collected directly from the center.
STEP 7 — IF CUSTOMER FORGOT DELIVERY OPTION: Ask them to check application paper or SMS received.
  They do NOT need to return — card will be delivered directly.
STEP 8 — IF URGENT: If urgent service was selected, card can be collected directly from the center.
